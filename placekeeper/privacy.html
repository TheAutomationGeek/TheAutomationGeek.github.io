Privacy policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · Applies to the PlaceKeeper apps for Windows and Android

In short

PlaceKeeper stores your inventory on your own devices. We, the people who make PlaceKeeper, never receive it. There are no accounts, no servers that hold your data, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What PlaceKeeper stores, and where

Your items, places, photos, loans and history are stored in the app's own database on each device you use. It is encrypted by default with a key held by your device's operating system; you can turn encryption off in Settings. Backups you create are encrypted with a passphrase you choose and saved wherever you put them.

Optional connections

PlaceKeeper only talks to an outside service if you turn on a feature that needs it:

Signing in to these services happens on the provider's own page. PlaceKeeper keeps the resulting access token on your device, protected by the operating system, and never shares it.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, PlaceKeeper uses Google's Drive permission only to create, read and update PlaceKeeper's own encrypted files in the folder you choose or that is shared with you, so that your devices (and, if you share a household, the people you share with) stay in step. PlaceKeeper does not read, use or modify any other files in your Drive. Google user data is used only to provide this sync feature on your devices; it is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not read by humans.

PlaceKeeper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Camera, photos and notifications

The camera is used only while you scan a label or barcode or take a photo of an item; images stay on your device. Reminders are worked out on your device and shown as local notifications.

Sharing with others

Nothing is shared unless you choose to: by printing labels or a report, exporting a backup or spreadsheet, or inviting someone to a shared household. Items you mark Private are left out of shared exports and their labels never show a name.

Children

PlaceKeeper is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its data from that device. Files in your own cloud folder can be deleted there at any time, and you can disconnect a cloud account in Settings. Disconnecting a Google account can also be done at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
